Python · FastAPI · React · ChromaDB · Ollama · Docker · DeepSeek / OpenAI / Claude

A support assistant over hardware manuals and internal specs, answering for 300+ customer accounts a week through an embeddable widget. Hybrid BM25 and dense retrieval merged with reciprocal rank fusion, a sigmoid-calibrated cross-encoder reranker, then an NLI entailment check on every sentence before it reaches the screen.

The bug I nearly shipped: the hallucination gate was passing invented claims at 0.99 confidence and refusing verbatim-correct ones at 0.02 — the verifier was reading the classifier's neutral class instead of entailment. Every surface metric looked healthy.

Correcting the class index and premise granularity took a true/false claim battery from three wrong verdicts to 8 out of 8.

I also A/B-tested two competing fixes for a retrieval fault on parallel isolated instances, and kept the narrower one that regressed nothing.

Facial age estimation · research

Six experiments. Two failures kept.

The fix wasn't more balancing. Age is ordinal, and the loss didn't know it.

  • 48.65 → 57.86%fine-age accuracy
  • 92.6%near-group
  • 274kfaces
Read the evidence

TensorFlow / Keras · EfficientNetB0 · VGG16 · WIKI / IMDB / UTKFace / FG-NET

A hierarchical multi-task model predicting gender, coarse age band, fine age band and continuous age from a face. The dataset is brutally imbalanced — 101,294 faces in the 34–48 band against 153 in the 3–5 band. Downsampling cost more in representation than it bought in balance; weighting the minority classes was worse still.

The problem wasn't imbalance: I'd encoded an ordinal variable as categorical. Soft ordinal labels took fine-age accuracy from 48.65% to 57.86%.
